Statutory Bodies

Key National Agencies & Mandates

These federal and civic institutions coordinate disaster risk reduction, meteorological forecasting, relief distribution, and medical crisis response across Nigeria.

🚨

NEMA

National Emergency Management Agency

The federal body coordinating disaster prevention, response, and post-climate relief. Manages emergency relief grain reserves, mobile camps, and rescue helicopters.

  • National Toll-Free: 0800-CALL-NEMA
  • National Emergency Line: 112 (All Networks)
  • Headquarters: Plot 439 Adetokunbo Ademola, Wuse II, Abuja
🌦️

NIMET

Nigerian Meteorological Agency

Provides daily weather advisories, seasonal climate predictions (SCP), dry spell warnings, and early warning radar alerts for agriculture and civil defense.

  • General Enquiries: 09-2913333 / 09-2913334
  • Website Portal: www.nimet.gov.ng
  • Headquarters: Bill Clinton Drive, Nnamdi Azikiwe Airport, Abuja
⛑️

Nigerian Red Cross

Humanitarian Relief & First Aid

Specialized in grassroot community rescue, emergency first aid, setting up temporary flood displacement tents, and water purification tablet distribution.

  • Disaster Helpline: 0803-700-0888 / 0802-300-4567
  • Presence: Branches in all 774 Local Government Areas (LGAs)
  • Website: redcrossnigeria.org
🏥

NCDC / FMOH

Disease Outbreak & Health Surveillance

Monitors El Niño-related outbreaks including Cholera in flood zones and Cerebrospinal Meningitis (CSM) in scorching Northern savannas.

  • NCDC Toll-Free: 6232 (Toll-Free)
  • WhatsApp Alert: 0708 711 0839
  • Headquarters: Plot 801 Ebitu Ukiwe St, Jabi, Abuja
🚒

Federal & State Fire

Wildfire & Electrical Infernos

Rapid mobilization for bushfires, market fires, and residential blazes exacerbated by 40°C+ ambient temperatures and dried vegetation.

  • National Fire Line: 112 or 0803-200-3556
  • State Fire Units: Operating in all State Capitals & Major Commercial Towns
  • Priority Alert: Do not attempt to fight massive bushfires with tree branches
🌾

FMAFS & Extension

Federal Ministry of Agriculture

Distributes subsidized drought-tolerant hybrid seeds, coordinates with Agricultural Development Programmes (ADPs), and provides post-drought compensation.

  • Farmers Hotline: 0800-FARM-NIGERIA
  • Seed Certification: National Agricultural Seeds Council (NASC)
  • Website: agriculture.gov.ng
Emergency Protocols

How to Seek Help in a Crisis

Knowing what exact information to provide during an emergency call dramatically cuts down response time for rescue teams.

Scenario 1

Trapped by Rapid Flash Floods

  1. Climb to the highest accessible structural point: (Roof, reinforced upper balcony, or strong tree). Avoid attics without escape hatches to the roof.
  2. Call 112 immediately: Clearly state: (a) Your State, LGA, and specific landmark/street name; (b) Number of people trapped, including infants or elderly; (c) Water level height (e.g., waist-level, chest-level).
  3. Signal rescue teams: Hang bright clothing (red, orange, yellow wrapper/shirt) on the exterior or turn on mobile phone torches intermittently.
  4. Do not attempt swimming: Never venture into muddy flood currents—submerged wires, debris, and undertows can overpower strong swimmers.
Scenario 2

Severe Heatstroke / Medical Collapse

  1. Move the victim immediately into shade: Take them indoors or under a dense tree away from direct sunlight.
  2. Rapid physical cooling: Loosen tight clothing; sponge their head, neck, armpits, and groin with cool (not ice-cold) water. Fan them vigorously.
  3. Administer fluids ONLY if conscious: If awake and coherent, give small sips of clean water or ORS. Never force liquids into an unconscious person.
  4. Transport to Primary Health Centre (PHC): Contact local Red Cross volunteers or transport immediately for IV saline therapy.
Scenario 3

Farmer Disaster Relief Application

  1. Document crop / livestock loss: Take clear photographs or video evidence of scorched crops, dry irrigation furrows, or dead poultry/livestock.
  2. Report to Local Government Council (LGA) Agric Officer: Request an official verification visit and damage assessment form.
  3. Register with your State Agricultural Development Programme (ADP): Ensure your Farmer ID number is attached to your cooperative union.
  4. Submit formal claim to NEMA / SEMA Disaster Relief Desk: Attach photos, community village head endorsement letter, and farm coordinate details.
Scenario 4

Community Mutual Aid & Alert Organizing

  1. Form a Community Climate Committee: Collaborate with your Community Development Association (CDA), Landlord Association, or Ward Elders.
  2. Identify vulnerable neighbors: Map out where elderly citizens living alone, disabled residents, and pregnant mothers reside for priority assistance.
  3. Set up an emergency whistle or megaphone warning: Establish a distinct whistle code for impending flood surges or approaching bushfires.
  4. Maintain clean neighborhood drains: Organize bi-weekly sanitation exercises to ensure stormwater drains stay unobstructed.
Citizen Helpdesk Simulation

Report a Local Climate Hazard

Help simulate rapid disaster reporting. Submit localized climate hazards (e.g. dried community borehole, blocked municipal canal, sudden brush fire, or heat exhaustion outbreak) to generate a tracked dispatch ticket.
